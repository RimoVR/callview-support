# Callview support

Callview is a native, read-only CT review companion for authorized CHILI
WebViewer users on iPhone and iPad. Your organization supplies and manages
your CHILI access. Callview does not create or change medical records and is
not a replacement for a diagnostic workstation.

## Getting started

1. Install Callview through the App Store link or TestFlight invitation supplied
   to you. The app is named **Callview**; the new native version is **2.0.0**.
2. Sign in with the CHILI account approved by your organization. If you cannot
   connect, ask your service administrator to check account access and any
   required network or VPN connection.
3. Select a study and series. Use the viewer toolbar for scrolling, window and
   level, zoom, pan and measurements. The **?** help lists touch gestures and
   other controls.

After moving from version 1, you may need to sign in again and re-enter local
viewer preferences. Updating the app does not grant additional CHILI access.

## Accounts and privacy

Callview has no registration or account-administration service. Contact your
organization for account creation, password reset, access changes, or deletion
of records held by its CHILI service.

Signing out ends the clinical session. To remove remembered credentials from
this device, use **Gespeicherten Zugang vergessen** on the login screen. This
does not delete the account at your organization. Do not rely on uninstalling
the app to erase credentials retained by the operating system's secure store.

The bundled offline demonstration contains licensed veterinary Greyhound CT
images, with no human patient data. Its images and attribution are included
locally. See the [privacy policy](privacy-policy.md) for live-session handling.

## Help with a problem

Email [pmk@pmn.de](mailto:pmk@pmn.de), or open a
[support issue](https://github.com/RimoVR/callview-support/issues). Include the
Callview version/build, device model, OS version and steps that reproduce the
problem. The app version appears in **?** help.

**GitHub issues are public.** Do not include patient information, clinical
screenshots, names, study identifiers, reports, passwords, CHILI URLs or other
confidential information. Use the offline demonstration for screenshots. The
developer does not need your patient records or password to handle a bug report.

This repository contains public support and privacy information only. It does
not contain the Callview application source or clinical data.

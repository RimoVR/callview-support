# Callview Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 19 August 2026**

Callview is a read-only CT review client for authorized CHILI WebViewer access.

## Information handled

When a user signs in to an authorized CHILI service, Callview processes the
credentials, session state, study metadata, and image data needed to display
the requested review. This can include health information supplied by that
service. The data is handled only for the signed-in session and is not sold,
used for advertising, or used to build a profile.

Credentials are stored only in the platform's protected credential store.
Session data and patient data remain in memory. Compressed frame data may be
held temporarily in an encrypted, application-private cache; its key exists
only for the current session. Signing out, a clean exit, or the next launch
after a crash removes that temporary cache.

Callview does not include advertising SDKs, analytics, tracking, or social
sign-in. It does not write studies or reports back to CHILI.

## Network and service providers

Callview connects only to the CHILI WebViewer service configured by the
authorized user. The service operator controls that service and its data
handling. Callview does not send patient data to the developer or to an
analytics provider.

## Your choices and contact

Use the account controls of the authorized CHILI service to request access,
correction, or deletion of data held by that service. Remove the Callview app
to remove its local application data. For questions, open a
[support issue](https://github.com/RimoVR/callview-support/issues) or email
[pmk@pmn.de](mailto:pmk@pmn.de).

This policy may be updated when the app's data practices change.
